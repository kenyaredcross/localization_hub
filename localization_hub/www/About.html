About the Localisation Alliance

A journey toward National Society self-reliance through collaborative leadership, shared vision, and coordinated action across Africa.

What is the Localisation Alliance?

The Localisation Alliance is a collaborative platform bringing together African National Societies committed to advancing stronger, locally led humanitarian and development systems. It provides a space for aligned leadership, shared vision, and coordinated action to accelerate localisation efforts across the continent.

Mission: To build sustainable, locally led humanitarian systems through peer collaboration, institutional strengthening, and shared learning among African National Societies.

The Alliance operates as more than just a project — it represents a fundamental shift toward African-led humanitarian action, ensuring that local expertise and leadership drive the response to community needs.

Anchored in Global Commitments

The Localisation Alliance's work is explicitly linked to key global and regional mandates, ensuring institutional credibility and alignment with the broader humanitarian ecosystem:

  • The Grand Bargain and IFRC NSD Compact — Commitments to strengthen National Society capacity and increase direct funding to local actors
  • IFRC Strategy 2030 — Building stronger National Societies as the foundation of the Red Cross Red Crescent Movement
  • Agenda for Renewal — Ensuring National Societies are accountable, transparent, and sustainable
  • Pan-African Conference (PAC) 2013 & 2017 — Regional commitments to strengthen African National Societies' autonomy and capacity

Leadership & Governance

The Alliance operates through a Steering Group that provides strategic direction, coordination, and oversight. The group ensures transparency, accountability, and collective decision-making to achieve commitments under the Alliance's guiding principles.

The Localisation Hub

The Localisation Hub, currently hosted by the Kenya Red Cross Society (KRCS), serves as the implementing arm of the Alliance. It translates the Alliance's vision into practice through coordination, technical support, learning facilitation, and project implementation.

Current Hub Initiatives

Digital Transformation

  • Development of this digital platform for continuous learning
  • Supporting digital transformation across National Societies
  • Systems strengthening in African National Societies

Impact & Measurement

  • Conducting Financial Sustainability Impact Studies
  • Designing measurement frameworks for African NS contexts
  • Tracking progress through key indicators

Capacity Building

  • Establishing an NSD Working Group in Africa
  • Developing peer-to-peer learning assessment tools
  • Facilitating knowledge exchange between societies

Guiding Principles

The Alliance is anchored in the following principles that guide all our work and decision-making:

Locally Led Decision-Making

African National Societies lead the agenda, set priorities, and own the outcomes. Decisions are made by those closest to the communities served.

Mutual Accountability

All members hold each other accountable to shared commitments, transparent processes, and collective goals.

Sustainability

Building long-term resilience through diversified resources, strong governance, and institutional development.

Peer-to-Peer Collaboration

Learning from each other's experiences, sharing solutions, and building together as equals.

Institutional Strengthening

Strengthening leadership, governance, and operational capacities across all levels of National Societies.

Knowledge Sharing

Documenting, sharing, and scaling successful approaches across the continent.

Primary Areas of Focus

The Alliance concentrates its efforts on three interconnected areas that are critical to National Society self-reliance:

Financial Sustainability

Building long-term financial resilience through:

  • Resource mobilisation strategies
  • Income diversification
  • Domestic fundraising capacity
  • Financial planning and management

Institutional Development

Strengthening organizational capacity through:

  • Leadership and governance
  • Branch development
  • Digital transformation
  • Systems and processes

Knowledge Sharing

Facilitating learning and collaboration through:

  • Regional peer exchange
  • Tool and resource documentation
  • Best practice sharing
  • Community of practice
