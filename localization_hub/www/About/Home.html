About the Localisation Alliance

The Localisation Alliance is a collaborative platform bringing together African National Societies and Consortium Partners, committed to advancing stronger, locally led humanitarian and development systems across the continent.

Every national society has adapted something. A training guide. A first aid poster. A volunteer script. Localisation Hub is where that work lives, so the next society doesn't start from zero.

Explore the Alliance

Four Pillars · One Journey

The Alliance's work is organized around four technical pillars, each led by a specific partner to ensure a holistic path toward National Society self-reliance.

Leadership & Governance

Strengthening institutional leadership, governance capacities, and effective decision-making processes across National Societies.

Branch Development

Building strong, sustainable branches with progressive activities and comprehensive development frameworks.

Domestic Resource Mobilisation

Diversifying income sources, including Workplace First Aid initiatives and sustainable revenue streams.

Finance Development

Establishing robust financial systems, core cost policies, and sustainable financial management.

Hub Initiatives

The Localisation Hub translates the Alliance's vision into practice through coordination, technical support, and implementation.

Digital Transformation

Hosted by Kenya Red Cross

Development of this digital platform for continuous learning, plus digital systems strengthening across African National Societies.
